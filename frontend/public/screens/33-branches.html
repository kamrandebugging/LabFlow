Multi-Branch Specimen Transfer Protocol & Branch Code Immutability

In accordance with ISO 15189:2022 laboratory traceability standards, Branch Identifier codes (CDL-*) are permanently sealed once committed to thermal specimen barcode ribbons or patient invoices. Specimens transferred between satellite collection facilities and the central Gulberg testing bench preserve their accession barcode and generate automated custody handshakes upon courier arrival.
